Eazybe credits are funds stored in the workspace wallet and used for eligible paid WhatsApp API activity. From **Settings → Billing → Credits**, you can check the current balance, make a one-time purchase, or manage auto payment when it is available.

## What the Credits Tab Shows

| Area | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Current balance** | The wallet funds currently available to the workspace |
| **Auto payment** | Whether automatic top-ups are active and, when expanded, the saved threshold and recharge amount |
| **Add credits** | Quick amounts, a custom amount field, the expected new balance, and **Complete purchase** |

<Info>
  A one-time credit purchase and an automatic top-up both add funds to the same workspace wallet. Auto payment does not replace your Eazybe subscription payment.
</Info>

## If the Balance Runs Out

Paid broadcasts, templates, or automations can stop when the wallet no longer has enough funds for the activity. Add credits or resolve a failed auto payment, then retry the affected action after the balance updates.

<Note>
  Access to **Credits** depends on your workspace permissions and eligible Eazybe services. Ask a workspace admin if the tab is not visible.
</Note>
