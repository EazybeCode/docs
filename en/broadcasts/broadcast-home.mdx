Use **Broadcasts** to review campaign performance, compare templates or sender numbers, and export the results you need. The **Broadcast**, **Template**, and **Number** controls determine how the table groups your results.

![Broadcasts page with the grouping controls and sender selector highlighted in violet](/images/broadcast-custom-filter/broadcast-overview.png)

<Warning>
  These screenshots were captured from a local workspace for this guide. The campaign names, dates, and totals belong to that workspace; your report will show your own data.
</Warning>

## Before You Start

Open **Broadcasts** in your workspace. You need a WhatsApp Business number available in the workspace to review its campaigns. If you see **Connect to WABA**, [connect a WhatsApp Business number](/en/waba/connect-waba) first.

Filters, grouping, sorting, and exports help you review existing activity. They do not send a new message or change a contact's preferences. To start a campaign, select **New broadcast** and follow [Create a Broadcast](/en/broadcasts/create-broadcast).

## Choose Your View

| View | What each row represents | Available filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Broadcast** | An individual campaign; select its name to open its analytics | Sender number, templates, template category, and campaign **Status** |
| **Template** | A template with its broadcast count, message totals, and cost | Sender number and templates, including selection by category |
| **Number** | A sender number with its broadcast count, message totals, and cost | One or more numbers and a date range |

## Understand the Columns

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Broadcast** | Campaign name. Its template and category appear underneath; select the name with **↗** to open the campaign. |
| **Template** | Template name, with its category underneath. This view combines activity for that template. |
| **Number** | Sender name, with the phone number underneath. This view combines activity for that sender. |
| **Broadcasts** | Number of campaigns included in a template or number summary. |
| **Total** | Total message/recipient records reported for the row. |
| **Sent** | Messages whose reported delivery status is Sent. |
| **Delivered** | Messages whose reported delivery status is Delivered. |
| **Read** | Messages whose reported delivery status is Read. |
| **Failed** | Messages reported as failed. Open the campaign to review available reasons. |
| **Cost** | Recorded cost for the row, shown using the workspace currency setting. |
| **Date** | Latest available campaign activity date, or the sent date when a later activity date is unavailable. **--** means no valid date is available. |
| **Status** | Campaign state, such as **Completed**, **In-Progress**, or **Failed**. |

**Date** and **Status** are available in **Broadcast** view. **Broadcasts** is available in **Template** and **Number** views. Use **Columns** to show a field that is hidden.

<Note>
  A campaign's status and its recipients' delivery statuses answer different questions. For example, a **Completed** campaign can still have failed recipients. Use **Failed** inside that campaign for the reasons. Summary totals can include the same contact across different campaigns; they are not a count of unique people across your workspace.
</Note>

<Note>
  Date-range controls appear only in **Number** view. Although **Status** also remains visible in **Template** view, use **Broadcast** view to filter individual campaigns by status.
</Note>

## Build Your Report

- [Filter Broadcasts and Templates](/en/broadcasts/filter-broadcasts-and-templates): choose a sender, select templates or a category, and narrow campaigns by status.
- [Compare Numbers by Date](/en/broadcasts/compare-numbers-by-date): choose sender numbers and use a preset or custom date range.
- [Customize and Export Reports](/en/broadcasts/customize-and-export-reports): choose columns, sort rows, and download a CSV.

## Refresh or Start Over

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Refresh** | Requests updated results using your current selections. Wait for loading to finish before reading or exporting the totals. |
| **Reset filters** in **Broadcast** or **Template** | Clears template, category, and status restrictions, keeps the current sender, and refreshes the report. |
| **Reset filters** in **Number** | Selects all listed numbers, clears the date range and any retained template/status restrictions, and refreshes the report. |
| **Remove date filter** | Clears only the date restriction and keeps the chosen numbers. |

Column choices stay as you set them when you reset filters. Switching between **Broadcast**, **Template**, and **Number** clears the selected sort order. Your number/date choices and sender/template choices are separate; a number date range does not filter the campaign or template view.

## Open an Individual Campaign

In **Broadcast** view, select the campaign name with the **↗** icon to open its detailed analytics. Use [View Broadcast Analytics](/en/broadcasts/view-analytics) for the recipient-level report.

<Tip>
  Select **Refresh** to request the latest data. If a loading error says your last successful data is still shown, the visible totals may be out of date; use **Retry** in the error message or **Refresh** once the connection is available.
</Tip>
