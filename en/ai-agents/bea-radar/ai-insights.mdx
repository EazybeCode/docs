## The AI Sales Brief

On every chat — on any number type, and in 1:1 and group conversations alike — BEA Radar automatically fills in a ready-to-act brief:

| Property | What it captures |
|:---------|:-----------------|
| **AI Summary** | A plain-language recap of the whole conversation — no scrolling |
| **Intent** | What the person wants (e.g. *Wants a demo*, *Ready to buy*, *Just browsing*) |
| **Urgency** | How time-sensitive the conversation is |
| **Objection** | What's holding the deal back, if anything |
| **Next Action** | The exact next step the rep should take |

No setup, no rules, no tagging — the brief is written the moment the chat comes in, and updates as the conversation continues.

<Note>
  The brief is written for **Personal WhatsApp**, **WhatsApp Business App**, and **WhatsApp Business API (WABA)** numbers alike, and group chats get the same treatment as direct ones.
</Note>

## Viewing AI Insights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Inbox">
    Click on **"Team Inbox"** in the navigation menu.

    ![Click on Team Inbox](/images/bea-radar-1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a Chat and View the User Profile">
    Open any chat, then open the **User Profile** panel on the right.

    ![Open a chat and open the User Profile panel](/images/bea-radar-2.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="See Your AI Properties">
    Here are your **AI Properties** — automatically created based on what your business tracks.

    ![AI Properties panel in the user profile](/images/bea-radar-3.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Your AI Insights">
    View your **AI Insights** — Summary, Intent, Urgency Reason, Next Action, and more — all analysed by BEA Radar.

    ![AI Insights showing Summary, Intent, Urgency Reason and Next Action](/images/bea-radar-4.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The brief refreshes as the conversation continues, so reopening a chat later always shows the current state — not what it looked like when the chat first arrived.
</Tip>
