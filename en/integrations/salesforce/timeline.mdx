With the new EazyBe Team Inbox package, your WhatsApp conversations are synced directly to the **Timeline** of your Salesforce records. Every message appears as a timeline entry, so the complete WhatsApp history of a contact is visible right where your team already works.

<Note>
This Timeline flow is part of the **new EazyBe Team Inbox package** and applies to new installations. Existing orgs that don't install the new package are not affected — their chats keep syncing to the previous locations (**Notes & Attachments** and the **WhatsApp Chats by EazyBe** object) exactly as before.
</Note>

## Where to See the Timeline

Open any **Contact**, **Lead**, or **Person Account** record in Salesforce. The **Timeline** on the record page shows all WhatsApp conversations with that record, in chronological order.

## What You'll See

Each synced WhatsApp message appears on the Timeline as a dedicated entry with:

- **Direction** — whether the message was sent or received
- **Sender and receiver** — who exchanged the message
- **Timestamp** — when the message was sent
- **Message content** — the full conversation text

  <img src="/images/salesforce-timeline-entry.png" alt="Details of a WhatsApp message entry on the Timeline" />

<Tip>
The Timeline works hand in hand with the [EazyBe Team Inbox](/en/integrations/salesforce/team-inbox): the Team Inbox shows the live conversation view, while the Timeline keeps the permanent history on the record.
</Tip>

## Requirements

To see WhatsApp conversations on the Timeline, you need:

- The **EazyBe Team Inbox package** installed in your Salesforce org
- The **prerequisite setup** completed (Record Types, Sharing Settings, and Field History Tracking)
- The EazyBe record pages **activated** for Contact, Lead, and Person Account

New to the setup? Follow the full guide in [Team Inbox](/en/integrations/salesforce/team-inbox).
