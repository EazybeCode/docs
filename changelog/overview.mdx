# Changelog

Latest updates and improvements to Eazybe

<Update label="October 30, 2026">
### Added - Eazybe MCP

Run Eazybe from **Claude**, **ChatGPT** and **Claude Code**, just by asking.

- Ask AI to find chats needing replies, send templates, and create deals.
- Available on paid plans with secure login; connect through Settings → MCP connection.
</Update>

<Update label="October 29, 2026">
### Added - Eazybe Android App

Eazybe is now on Android. Reply to chats from the Team Inbox and manage your CRM on the go.

- Download from the Google Play Store
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026">
### Added - Virtual Numbers

Buy virtual numbers inside Eazybe. Sold as an add-on to your plan.

- Use virtual numbers for calling and WhatsApp, with a call button in the Team Inbox.
- Numbers are available in the US, Canada, and Puerto Rico, with incoming OTP SMS and verification handled by Eazybe.

### Added - Marketing Template Bid Cap

Control the maximum price you pay per delivered marketing message.

- Set a base bid and override it for individual countries.
- Preview how many people your bid can reach.

### Added - 6-Month WhatsApp Chat Backup

Connect your WhatsApp Business number with Coexistence and Eazybe imports up to 6 months of chat history. Keep using the WhatsApp app on your phone as usual.
</Update>

<Update label="January 11, 2026">
### Added - AI Unreplied Chats Agent

We've introduced intelligent chat prioritization to help your team focus on what matters most.

- Prioritize chats using sentiment, urgency, and CRM customer value.
- Send admins a WhatsApp summary every three hours.

### Added - Enhanced Privacy Controls

New privacy and compliance features to keep your data secure:

- Access privacy documentation and GDPR tools with DPA support.
- Manage employee-monitoring transparency and exclude labeled chats from backup.
</Update>

<Update label="January 5, 2026">
### Added - AI Reply Suggestions

Get AI-powered message suggestions to respond faster and more professionally.

- Choose a reply tone and insert suggestions into any WhatsApp conversation.
- Message data is not stored after processing.

### Fixed - HubSpot Sync Improvements

- Improved contact matching and attachment syncing.
- Reduced average sync delay from five minutes to three.
</Update>
