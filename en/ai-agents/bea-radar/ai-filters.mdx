## What AI Filters Do

Search and filter your **entire inbox** by any AI property — default or custom.

- *"Show every hot lead with no objection left."*
- *"Every chat with high urgency waiting on a reply."*
- *"All deals interested in the Team Plan."*

Instead of scrolling hundreds of conversations, the right ones surface in one click.

## Filtering Your Inbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Filters">
    Click on **"AI filters"** to filter your chat list based on an AI property.

    ![AI filters button above the chat list](/images/bea-radar-9.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an AI Property">
    Select the AI property you want to filter by — for example, **"Conversation State"**.

    ![Selecting the Conversation State AI property](/images/bea-radar-10.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Property Value">
    Then select the property value — for example, **"Waiting on Rep"**.

    ![Selecting the Waiting on Rep property value](/images/bea-radar-11.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add More Filters">
    In the same way, you can add more filters to build a tighter, more optimised chat list.

    ![Combining multiple AI filters](/images/bea-radar-12.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AI filters can be combined with the standard chat filters for an even more optimised list.
</Note>
