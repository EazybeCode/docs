title: "Set Up Auto Payment"
description: "Automatically add credits when an eligible Eazybe wallet reaches a low-balance threshold."
---

Auto payment charges a saved card and adds credits when the workspace wallet reaches your chosen threshold. Use it to reduce interruptions to eligible paid WhatsApp activity.

## Turn On Auto Payment

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Credits**.
2. Expand **Auto payment** if the panel is collapsed.
3. Turn on the **Auto payment** toggle.
4. Confirm **Top up when below** and **Amount to add**.
5. Confirm that the status changes to **Auto payment active**.

If this is the first setup, Eazybe uses the eligible card already saved for the workspace subscription.

<Warning>
  Turning on auto payment authorizes future charges to the saved card. If Eazybe cannot find a usable subscription card, auto payment is not enabled. Update the subscription payment method or contact Eazybe Support, then try again.
</Warning>

## Choose the Amounts

- **Top up when below** is the wallet balance that triggers auto payment.
- **Amount to add** is charged to the saved card and added to the wallet after a successful payment.
- The threshold must be lower than the amount to add.

For a USD wallet, the threshold must be at least USD 1, the amount to add must be at least USD 5, and each value can be up to USD 10,000.

Example: if **Top up when below** is USD 5 and **Amount to add** is USD 50, Eazybe starts an automatic payment when the balance reaches USD 5 or less. A successful payment adds USD 50.

<Warning>
  The example explains the trigger; it is not a recommended setting. Choose amounts that match your expected messaging usage and budget.
</Warning>

## Change the Amounts

1. Select an amount field or its pencil icon.
2. Update **Top up when below** and **Amount to add**.
3. Select **Save changes**.
4. Recheck the summary shown in the panel.

## Change the Payment Card

1. Select **Change card** beside the saved card.
2. Enter the new card details on the secure Stripe page and complete the setup.
3. Return to **Credits** and confirm that the card brand and last four digits have updated.

Eazybe displays only the saved card brand and last four digits in the workspace.

## Turn Auto Payment Off or On Again

- To stop future automatic top-ups, turn off the toggle and confirm **Disable auto payment**.
- Disabling keeps the saved configuration so you can turn it on again later.
- Check the status text to confirm whether auto payment is active.

<Warning>
  Turning auto payment off does not add credits or cancel a payment that has already started. Check **Wallet history** when a payment was triggered near the time you disabled it.
</Warning>

## Emails and Notifications

Auto-payment activity is sent to the workspace account email and can also create an in-app notification.

| Event | What you receive |
| --- | --- |
| Payment started | Amount and transaction ID |
| Payment successful | Amount, transaction ID, and updated balance when available |
| Payment failed | Failure reason, retry timing, and instructions for updating the payment method |
| Repeated failures | Auto payment is disabled after three failed attempts and a final email is sent |
