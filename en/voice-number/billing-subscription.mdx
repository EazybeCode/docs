Eazybe Voice Number lets you make and receive calls and receive SMS where supported. The prices shown here are Eazybe’s customer rates, separate from your workspace plan:

- **Number rental:** A recurring monthly charge for your Voice Number.
- **Usage:** Calls and incoming SMS are deducted from your wallet.

## Monthly number rental

- Choose a country below to check rental prices. A range means available numbers have different rentals.
- Before buying, review the **amount due, applicable tax, and renewal date** on the purchase screen.

## Calling rates by country

1. Search and select your **Voice Number country**.
2. Select a **Destination country**, or check **Call within the same country** to fill it automatically.
3. Select **Check rates**. Switch between **USD** and **INR** as needed.

<VoiceUsagePricing />

## How call charges work

- **Outgoing:** Rates depend on your number country and the destination.
- **Incoming:** Rates apply to the country of your Voice Number.
- **Final charge:** Deducted after the call ends and billing is processed. It includes both agent and customer call legs and depends on billed duration and call details.
- **India:** Indian Voice Numbers can call India only.

<Note>
The checker shows starting rates before tax. **Wallet history** shows the final amount charged in your wallet currency. An unavailable rate does not mean free usage.
</Note>

## Wallet balance and top-up

- To start a call, keep at least **₹100 in an INR wallet** or **$1 in a USD wallet**.
- This is the required minimum balance, not the price of a call. Number rental is separate.

To add credits:

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Credits**.
2. Choose an amount under **Add credits** and complete payment.
3. Check **Wallet history** for the confirmed recharge.

## SMS charges

- **Incoming SMS** is charged per message part, where supported. See the rate in the checker above.
- Longer messages use multiple parts, each billed separately.
- **Outgoing SMS** is not currently offered.

## Where to check your charges

- **Wallet deductions and recharges:** Settings → Billing → Wallet history.
- **Individual calls:** CALLS → Call History.
- **Number rental and renewal:** CALLS → Phone Numbers → Your numbers.
