---
title: "SMS"
description: "Read incoming SMS and verification codes, filter messages, and send an SMS from your voice number."
---

SMS keeps incoming and outgoing messages, including verification codes, in one place. You can filter by voice number and send a new message from an active number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SMS">
    Open **SMS** under **CALLS**. The page shows **Every message. Every code. In one place.** and an **All messages** list.

    ![SMS message list](/images/voice-number/sms-list.jpg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter or refresh messages">
    Use the number filter to choose **All numbers** or one active voice number. Click **Refresh messages** to fetch the latest messages. The table shows **From**, **To**, **Message**, **Status**, and **Sent Time**. Times are displayed in IST.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a verification code">
    Verification-code messages appear with the incoming messages for the related number. Use the eye icon to view the full message. When a current code is available, use **Copy verification code**. Expired codes are marked as too old to show.

    <Note>Opening an unread verification-code message marks it as read.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the SMS composer">
    Click **Send SMS**. In **New message**, choose an active number in **From**, enter the recipient in **To**, and type your message in **Message**. The recipient must include a country code, for example `+14155552671`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Keep the message within **1,600 characters**, then click **Send SMS**. Eazybe shows the message status in the list. To close the composer and keep your draft, use **Close and keep draft**. To discard it, use **Discard draft**.

    <Warning>Sending SMS is a real message to the recipient and may incur messaging charges. Check the number and message before clicking **Send SMS**.</Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>
