Team Inbox needs at least one connected WhatsApp number and the correct workspace access. Admins control the integration and permissions; each member then sees only the conversations and tools available to them.

## Connect the first WhatsApp number

If an admin or manager opens Team Inbox before a number is connected, Eazybe displays a connection screen with two choices:

| Connection | Choose it when | What is required |
|---|---|---|
| **Chrome extension** | You want to sync a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business App number. | Install the Eazybe extension and connect the number. The setup screen describes this as a sync that can be completed in under 30 seconds. |
| **WhatsApp Business API** | You want to use an official WABA number, templates, and the API messaging workflow. | A Meta Business account and a connected WhatsApp Business API number. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Inbox">
    Click **Team Inbox** in the main navigation. When no number is connected, the setup screen displays **Install Chrome extension** and **Connect WhatsApp Business API**.

## Team Inbox permissions

Admins configure member access in **Members & Access**. The relevant permissions are:

| Permission | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Team Inbox** | Whether the member can open Team Inbox. |
| **See all channels** | Whether the member can see every allowed channel or is limited to the WhatsApp numbers mapped to them. |
| **Broadcasts** | Whether broadcast-related lists and actions are available. |
| **User Properties** | Whether the member can edit customer and connected CRM information. Without it, supported information remains view-only. |

<Warning>
  Enabling **Team Inbox** does not automatically give a member access to every WhatsApp number. Check both **See all channels** and the number mapping.
</Warning>

## How roles affect the interface

Permissions can be customized, but the interface also applies role-based limits.

| Capability | Admin | Manager | Employee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open Team Inbox | Based on Team Inbox permission | Based on Team Inbox permission | Based on Team Inbox permission |
| See channels | All or permission-scoped | Can be team/channel-scoped | Commonly limited to mapped channels unless broader access is granted |
| Assign or unassign a WABA chat | Available | Available when the chat is in scope | Not shown |
| Add or remove favorites | Available | Available | Not available |
| Start a new chat | Available | Available when access is ready | Not shown |
| Use saved views | Available | Available | Not shown |
| See acquisition and AI-agent lists | Available when configured | Available when configured and in scope | Not shown |
| Manage AI-property definitions | Available on eligible plans | Not available | Not available |
| Edit allowed customer/CRM data | Based on User Properties permission | Based on User Properties permission | Based on User Properties permission |

Managers can be restricted to their team and approved channels. In AI-agent lists, stage counts may be hidden even when the manager can open the relevant stage.

## Why two members can see different conversations

A conversation appears only when all applicable access checks pass:

1. Team Inbox is enabled for the workspace.
2. The member has Team Inbox access.
3. The source WhatsApp number is available to that member.
4. A team-scoped manager is allowed to see the relevant member or channel.
5. The current list, search, and filters include the conversation.

The same checks also control which channels and assignees appear in filter menus.

## Security and safe collaboration

- Access changes affect what a member can see; they do not delete the underlying conversation.
- Assignment gives an owner responsibility for the chat but does not move or erase its history.
- Team Inbox displays sender information on outgoing messages so teammates can understand who replied.
- A disconnected WABA becomes read-only in Team Inbox until it is reconnected.

<Tip>
  After changing mapping or permissions, ask the member to reopen Team Inbox. If the number is still missing, verify the number's connection and synchronization settings.
</Tip>
