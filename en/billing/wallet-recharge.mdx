---
title: "Wallet recharge"
description: "Add credits to your Eazybe wallet with a secure one-time payment."
---

Use wallet recharge when you want to add credits once without changing your auto-payment settings.

## Add credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Credits">
    Go to **Settings → Billing → Credits** and find **Add credits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Select a quick amount or enter a custom amount. Eazybe shows the balance you will have after a successful purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to checkout">
    Click **Complete purchase** and review the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Finish the payment in the secure checkout. Your wallet balance updates after the payment succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Add credits using a custom amount](/images/billing/add-credits.jpeg)

## Payment flow by wallet currency

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="INR wallet">
    Enter an amount from INR 50 to INR 50,000. You can add or confirm GST details, review the tax summary, and complete payment through Razorpay.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Other currencies">
    Enter an amount from USD 1 to USD 10,000 for a USD wallet, or follow the range shown on your screen. Complete payment through Stripe.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
A one-time credit purchase does not create auto payment or change its threshold and recharge amount. Manage those settings separately in **Auto payment**.
</Note>
