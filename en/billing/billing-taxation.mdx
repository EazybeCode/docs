Use **Billing & taxation** to save your business GST information for INR payments and invoices.

<Note>
This tab is available to workspace admins for India-based billing.
</Note>

## Add GST details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & taxation">
    Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing & taxation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter and verify GSTIN">
    Enter the 15-character GSTIN and complete verification. Eazybe fills the registered company name, state, and address when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review billing information">
    Check the company name, state, and billing address. Add a finance email if invoices should go to a separate address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the details. They will be used for future eligible INR purchases and tax invoices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

During an INR credit purchase, Eazybe shows the GST breakdown before payment. Saved GST details help create a business tax invoice that may be used to claim Input Tax Credit where eligible.

For tax eligibility or accounting advice, consult your tax professional.
