Team Inbox brings the WhatsApp conversations your team is allowed to access into one workspace. From here, you can find a customer, read the complete message history, assign an owner, send replies, use approved WhatsApp templates, and keep customer context beside the conversation.

What you see depends on your role, your workspace plan, the WhatsApp numbers mapped to you, and the permissions configured in **Members & Access**.

## Understand the workspace

Team Inbox is divided into four areas:

| Area | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Open queues such as All conversations, Favorites, Assigned, Unassigned, Closing window, AI labels, advertisements, broadcasts, or AI-agent stages. |
| **Conversation list** | Search, filter, open, and review conversations. Each row can show the channel, last message, time, owner, unread state, priority, favorite, and WhatsApp messaging-window status. |
| **Conversation** | Read the history, see system events and delivery status, change the viewing number, assign an owner, and send messages. |
| **Customer details** | Work with the Eazybe profile, AI properties, follow-ups, tasks, and—when connected—HubSpot. |

You can collapse **Lists** to create more space. Select **Lists** above the conversation list to open it again. Opening customer details also makes the central workspace more compact.

## Important availability rules

- **WhatsApp source:** Some controls apply only to WhatsApp Business API (WABA) conversations, while app-synced conversations use the regular message composer.
- **Role and access:** Assignment, favorites, new chats, saved views, acquisition lists, AI labels, AI agents, broadcasts, and editable customer properties can be limited by role or permission.
- **Mapped channels:** A member can only see channels made available through Team Inbox access and number mapping.
- **Plan:** AI labels, AI profile intelligence, and AI-property features require an eligible plan. Free and Starter workspaces do not show those AI sections.
- **Connected integrations:** HubSpot tools appear only when HubSpot is connected. A disconnected WABA can still show history but cannot send replies until it is reconnected.

<Tip>
  If another user sees a different Team Inbox, first compare their role, **Members & Access** permissions, mapped WhatsApp numbers, workspace plan, and connected integrations.
</Tip>
