---

Use this guide to configure a HubSpot workflow that sends a WhatsApp template and supports a reply-based workflow through the Eazybe integration.

## Prerequisites

Before setting up your workflow, ensure you have:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="HubSpot Account" icon="hubspot">
    An active HubSpot account with workflow access
  </Card>
  <Card title="Eazybe Integration" icon="plug">
    WhatsApp Integration by Eazybe installed in HubSpot
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create Your HubSpot Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Your HubSpot Account">
    Log in to your HubSpot account to begin setting up the workflow.

    ![Access HubSpot](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-access-account.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Automation">
    Click on the **Automation** button in the main navigation menu.

    ![Navigate to Automation](/images/1933597310.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Workflows">
    From the automation menu, select **Workflows** to access the workflow builder.

    ![Select Workflows](/images/1933597303.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Workflow">
    Click **Create workflow** to start building a new automation.

    ![Create Workflow](/images/1933597307.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose From Scratch">
    Select **From scratch** to create a custom workflow tailored to your needs.

    ![From Scratch](/images/1933597320.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Filter Criteria">
    Click **Met filter criteria** to define the conditions for workflow enrollment.

    ![Filter Criteria](/images/1933597313.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Workflow Object">
    Choose **Contacts**, **Deals**, or **Leads** as the object type for your workflow.

    ![Select a workflow object](/images/1933597309.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Contact Properties">
    Click on **Contact properties** to configure specific enrollment criteria.

    ![Contact Properties](/images/1933597302.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for Property">
    Search for the contact property you want to use as the enrollment trigger.

    ![Search Property](/images/1933597316.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Property Name">
    Select your desired property (for example, "Company name") from the list.

    ![Choose Property](/images/1933597306.png)

    <Info>
      You can use any contact property such as lifecycle stage, lead status, or custom properties you've created.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Conditions">
    Set the condition (e.g., "is equal to any of") and add the matching values under **Add values**.

    ![Configure Conditions](/images/1933597299.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proceed to Next Step">
    Click **Next** to continue to the re-enrollment settings.

    ![Click Next](/images/1933597301.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Re-enrollment">
    Toggle **Re-enroll** if you want contacts who already met the criteria to enter the workflow again.

    ![Re-enrollment](/images/1933597311.png)

    <Warning>
      Be cautious with re-enrollment settings to avoid sending duplicate messages to the same contacts.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Enrollment Settings">
    Click **Save and continue** to preserve your enrollment configuration.

    ![Save Settings](/images/1933597304.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add WhatsApp Integration">
    Scroll down in the actions panel and select **WhatsApp Integration by Eazybe**.

    ![WhatsApp Integration](/images/1933597323.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Template Action">
    Click **Send WhatsApp & Wait for Reply** to choose a pre-approved message template.

    ![Select the Send WhatsApp and Wait for Reply action](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-select-action.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recipient Phone Number">
    Click **Recipient Phone Number** and choose which HubSpot contact property to use: **Mobile phone number**, **Phone number**, or **WhatsApp phone number**.

    ![Recipient Phone Number field](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-recipient-phone-number.png)

    <Tip>
      Ensure phone numbers are stored in international format (e.g., +1234567890) for successful delivery.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Phone Number Property">
    Choose one of the available properties: **Mobile phone number**, **Phone number**, or **WhatsApp phone number**.

    ![Select a phone number property](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-select-phone-property.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose WABA Number">
    Select a WABA user from the available options.

    ![Choose a WABA number](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-choose-whatsapp-template.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Button Template">
    Select an approved button template from the available options for creating a condition branch.

    ![Choose button template](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-select-whatsapp-template.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Template Selection">
    Click **Save** to confirm your template selection.

    ![Save template selection](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-save-template-selection.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Branch">
    Click the **+** button and create a new branch.

    ![Create a new branch](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-create-new-branch.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Branch">
    Choose a branch type to **send contacts to branches**.

    ![Select a branch type](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-select-branch.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Property Value">
    Choose the **User WhatsApp Reply** property from **1. Eazybe: Send WhatsApp & Wait for Reply** for this branch.

    ![Select the User WhatsApp Reply property](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-select-property-value.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Branch Condition Value">
    Add a branch condition value based on the template button name.

    ![Add a branch condition value](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-branch-condition-value.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Branch Values">
    Click **Save** after adding the branch values.

    ![Save the branch values](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-save-branch-values.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Template in Condition Branch">
    Click the **+** button to add templates for each branch value. When a user clicks one of the template buttons, the corresponding template is triggered.

    ![Add a template to a condition branch](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-add-template-condition-branch.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Template">
    Click **Save** to save the template for the branch value.

    ![Save the template for a branch value](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-save-branch-template.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Turn On">
    Click **Review and turn on** after adding templates for all branch values.

    ![Review and turn on the workflow](/images/hubspot-wait-reply-review-and-turn-on.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final Review">
    Either click **Skip to turn on** or click **Next** to perform additional validation checks.

    ![Final Review](/images/1933597322.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate Workflow">
    Click **Turn on workflow** to activate your WhatsApp campaign automation.

    ![Turn On Workflow](/images/1933597319.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Workflow is Ready

Congratulations! Your HubSpot workflow is now configured to automatically send WhatsApp messages to contacts who meet your specified criteria.

<Check>
  Your workflow will now automatically trigger WhatsApp messages whenever a contact meets the enrollment criteria you defined.
</Check>
