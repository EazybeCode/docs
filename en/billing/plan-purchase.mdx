## Purchase or upgrade a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    In your Eazybe workspace, open **Settings → Billing**, then select **Your plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Upgrade">
    Click **Upgrade** and choose the plan and monthly or yearly billing option that fits your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the price">
    Check the total price, billing period, and seat details. For INR billing, GST is added at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Continue to the secure checkout. Your plan details update after payment is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage an existing plan

- **Edit seats:** Add or remove seats when this option is available for your plan.
- **Upgrade:** Move to another plan or billing period.
- **Manage Subscription:** Open the Stripe billing portal to manage subscription billing.

<Note>
**Manage Subscription** is shown only for customers outside India. India-based customers can use the available plan and seat actions or contact Eazybe Support for billing help.
</Note>
