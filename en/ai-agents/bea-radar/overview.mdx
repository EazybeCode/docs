BEA Radar reads each WhatsApp conversation in your Team Inbox and writes what it finds into structured fields on the chat: a plain-language summary, what the person wants, how time-sensitive the conversation is, what is holding it back, and the next step for the rep. Alongside those, it works out which fields your own business tracks, such as budget, plan interest, or travel dates, creates them, and keeps them filled. Every field is then written to the matching record in your CRM.

It works with every type of WhatsApp number: Personal WhatsApp, the WhatsApp Business App, and WhatsApp Business API (WABA). Group chats are covered the same as direct ones, and there is nothing to enable per number or per chat type.

<Note>
  **Requirements:** access to the **Team Inbox**, and a connected CRM if you want properties written back to contact and deal records. Adding, renaming, and deleting properties requires the **Admin** role. BEA Radar is available on Eazybe's AI plans.
</Note>

## How It Works

Each conversation moves through six stages.

| Stage | What happens |
|:------|:-------------|
| **Capture** | Incoming WhatsApp chats in the Team Inbox are observed in real time, across every connected number and in both 1:1 and group chats |
| **Read and analyse** | The conversation is interpreted for meaning rather than matched against keywords |
| **Fill in properties** | A structured set of fields is written on the chat |
| **Self-configure** | Company-specific properties worth tracking are detected and created |
| **Surface** | The **AI filters** control retrieves the chats that match those properties |
| **Sync** | Each property is written to the connected CRM record |

Analysis begins as soon as a message arrives. There is nothing to trigger.

## Where to Find It

BEA Radar sits inside the Team Inbox rather than in a separate dashboard.

- **In the chat:** the AI properties panel is pinned beside each conversation in the **Team Inbox**.
- **Configuration:** **Team Inbox → Settings**, for adding and managing properties.
- **Retrieval:** the **AI filters** control sits above the chat list.
- **Destination:** the connected CRM record.

## What BEA Radar Records

Three groups of properties are written on every chat.

| Group | What it holds | Where to read more |
|:------|:--------------|:-------------------|
| **The AI sales brief** | A fixed set of fields written on every conversation: summary, intent, urgency, objection, and next action | [AI Insights on a Chat](/en/ai-agents/bea-radar/ai-insights) |
| **Auto-created business properties** | Fields derived from your own conversations, so the set differs by business. A travel team gets destination and travel dates; a SaaS team gets plan interest and seat count | [AI Properties](/en/ai-agents/bea-radar/ai-properties) |
| **Admin-defined properties** | Fields an Admin adds, described in a sentence so the AI knows what to capture | [AI Properties](/en/ai-agents/bea-radar/ai-properties) |

Any of the three can be used as a filter across the whole inbox. See [AI Filters](/en/ai-agents/bea-radar/ai-filters).

## CRM Sync

Every property, whether part of the sales brief, auto-created, or added by an Admin, is written to the matching contact or deal record in the connected CRM. Records update as conversations continue, so no chat has to be logged by hand.

Supported CRMs are **HubSpot**, **Salesforce**, and **Zoho**, along with Eazybe's broader integration set.

Confirm your CRM connection before relying on sync, so properties land on the right contact and deal records.

## Behaviour to Be Aware Of

- **The auto-created set differs per workspace.** Properties are derived from your own conversations, so two businesses on BEA Radar see different fields. They appear over your first conversations rather than all at once.
- **The brief updates as the conversation continues.** Reopening a chat later shows the current state, not the state at the time the chat arrived.
- **New properties fill going forward.** A property added today is filled on relevant chats from that point on.
- **A property's description defines what is captured.** For admin-defined properties, the AI reads the description to decide what to look for.
- **Property management is Admin-only.** Employees can change a property's value on a chat, but cannot add, rename, or delete properties or their options.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause and resolution |
|:------|:---------------------|
| Properties appear in the Team Inbox but not on the CRM record | The CRM is not connected, or the chat's number has no matching contact or deal record. Check the connection and confirm the contact exists in the CRM. |
| A property cannot be renamed or deleted | Renaming, adding, and deleting properties and options is available to the **Admin** role only. Employees can change a property's value, not the property itself. |
| An AI filter returns no chats | No chat has a value for that property yet, or the stacked filters exclude one another. Remove filters one at a time to find the one emptying the list. |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it work on all my WhatsApp numbers?">
    Yes. Every type of WhatsApp number is covered: Personal WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business App, and WhatsApp Business API (WABA). There is nothing to enable per number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are group chats analysed too?">
    Yes. Group chats receive the same properties as direct conversations, with no separate setup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an employee change a property value?">
    Yes. Employees can change the value of a property on a chat. Adding, renaming, and deleting the property itself, and editing its dropdown options, requires the Admin role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to create the properties myself?">
    No. BEA Radar detects the properties your business tracks from your conversations and creates them. Adding your own is optional, for a field you want captured that the AI has not created.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
