When you connect your WhatsApp Business app to WABA and choose to share chat history, up to **6 months of your 1:1 chats** will sync to the **Team Inbox**. You can continue using your WhatsApp Business app alongside the Business Platform.

<Note>
  **Attachments older than 14 days will not be shown** in the Team Inbox, even when their messages are included in the six-month history. Group chat history is not included.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Screenshots use fictional names, phone numbers and a made-up QR code. Scan the QR code in your own connection window when setting up your account.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection in Eazybe">
    Log in to [app.eazybe.com](https://app.eazybe.com). On your first login, look for the six-month chat backup announcement and click **Connect to API**.

    ![Six-month chat backup announcement](/images/waba-six-month-backup/backup-announcement.png)

    You can also open **Channels**, find the WhatsApp number you want to connect, and click **Connect to API** beside it.

    ![Connect to API beside your WhatsApp number](/images/waba-six-month-backup/connect-to-api.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with Meta setup">
    The **Facebook Login for Business** window opens. Review the connection information and click **Continue**.

    ![Continue with Facebook Login for Business](/images/waba-six-month-backup/meta-continue.png)

    On **Add your WhatsApp phone number**, select the appropriate business account and enter or check the WhatsApp Business number you want to connect. Choose the verification method shown, then click **Next**. Complete any verification prompts that appear.

    ![Select your business account and WhatsApp number](/images/waba-six-month-backup/phone-number.png)

    On **Select the business assets to share with Eazybe**, select or confirm your **Business portfolio**, then click **Next**.

    ![Select your business portfolio](/images/waba-six-month-backup/business-portfolio.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Business Platform on your phone">
    When the **Import contacts and chat history** screen displays a QR code on your computer, leave that screen open.

    Open **WhatsApp Business** on the phone using the number you are connecting. Go to **Settings → Account → Business Platform**, then tap **Connect to the Business Platform**.

    ![Connect to the Business Platform on WhatsApp Business](/images/waba-six-month-backup/connect-business-platform.jpg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose to share your chat history">
    On **Share chat history**, you will see two options:

    - **Share all chats:** Shares up to 6 months of 1:1 chat history. Group chat history cannot be shared.
    - **Don't share chats:** Connects without sharing your previous chat history.

    Select **Share all chats**, then tap **Confirm** to include your six-month chat backup.

    ![Select Share all chats and tap Confirm](/images/waba-six-month-backup/share-chat-history.jpg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Use the scanner in WhatsApp Business to scan the QR code displayed in your own Eazybe connection window on the computer.

    ![Chat history import screen with an illustrative QR code](/images/waba-six-month-backup/scan-qr-code.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    When WhatsApp Business displays the connection confirmation for **Eazybe Software India Pvt Ltd**, tap **OK**. Return to the setup window on your computer and complete any remaining prompts.

    ![Connection confirmation with OK button](/images/waba-six-month-backup/connection-confirmed.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View Your Chats in the Team Inbox

After completing step 6, your chat history will sync to the **Team Inbox** in approximately **15–30 minutes**, depending on how many chats you have.

Once syncing is complete, open the **Team Inbox** in Eazybe to view your backed-up 1:1 conversations from the past 6 months. Attachments older than 14 days will not be shown, and group chat history is not included.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much chat history can I bring into Eazybe?

You can share up to 6 months of 1:1 chat history when connecting your WhatsApp Business app to WABA.

### Where will my backed-up chats appear?

Your imported chats will appear in the Eazybe Team Inbox.

### How long does the initial sync take?

After you complete the connection in step 6, syncing takes approximately **15–30 minutes**, depending on how many chats you have.

### Why can't I see an older attachment?

Attachments older than 14 days are not shown in the Team Inbox, even if the associated message is within the six-month backup period.

### Are group chats included?

No. This backup includes 1:1 conversations only.

### Which option should I select to import my previous chats?

Select **Share all chats** and tap **Confirm**. Selecting **Don't share chats** does not import your previous chat history.
