# BEA Radar — WhatsApp Sales Intelligence

**BEA Radar** is Eazybe's AI layer that turns every WhatsApp conversation into structured, actionable sales data — automatically.

The moment a message lands, the AI reads the conversation and writes a **sales brief** right beside the chat: what it's about, what the person wants, how urgent it is, and the exact next step. It goes further — it **works out the custom fields your business tracks and creates them on its own**, lets you **filter your entire inbox** by any of them, and **pushes everything to your CRM** with no manual entry.

<Note>
    **In one line:** your reps read chats — Eazybe reads the deal.
</Note>

Think of it as conversation intelligence built for WhatsApp: self-configuring, and living right inside the inbox your team already uses.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Sales and support teams run hundreds of WhatsApp chats a day. In that volume:

- The deal that's **about to close** looks identical to small talk in the chat list.
- Buying signals — *"send pricing," "we'll decide Friday"* — get **buried and forgotten**.
- Reps waste hours **scrolling long threads** to remember where a conversation stands.
- The **CRM is always out of date**, because nobody has time to log chats by hand.
- Managers have **no structured view** of what's actually happening across conversations.

BEA Radar removes the manual reading, tagging, and logging — so the signal surfaces on its own and the CRM stays current without effort.

---

## How It Works (End-to-End Flow)

```
  Every WhatsApp chat
          ↓
  AI reads the conversation
          ↓
  Writes a sales brief  +  auto-creates the fields your business tracks
          ↓
  You filter the inbox to the chats that matter
          ↓
  Everything syncs to your CRM — automatically
```

1. **Capture** — the AI observes incoming WhatsApp chats in the Team Inbox in real time.
2. **Read & analyse** — it interprets the conversation, not just keywords, but the actual meaning.
3. **Fill in properties** — it writes a structured set of fields on the chat.
4. **Self-configure** — it detects the company-specific properties worth tracking and creates them.
5. **Surface** — the AI Filter lets you retrieve exactly the chats you want.
6. **Sync** — every property is pushed to the connected CRM record.

## Core Capabilities

### The AI Sales Brief (default properties)

On every chat, the AI automatically fills in a ready-to-act brief:

| Property | What it captures |
|:---------|:-----------------|
| **AI Summary** | A plain-language recap of the whole conversation — no scrolling |
| **Intent** | What the person wants (e.g. *Wants a demo*, *Ready to buy*, *Just browsing*) |
| **Urgency** | How time-sensitive the conversation is |
| **Objection** | What's holding the deal back, if anything |
| **Next Action** | The exact next step the rep should take |

No setup, no rules, no tagging — the brief is written the moment the chat comes in, and updates as the conversation continues.

### Auto-Created Company Properties (zero setup)

The standout capability: **the AI creates the properties your business needs, on its own.**

It studies your conversations, works out what your team actually tracks, and **generates those custom fields automatically** — then keeps them filled on every chat.

- A **real estate** team gets fields like *Property Type* and *Budget*.
- A **SaaS** team gets *Plan Interest* and *Seat Count*.
- A **travel** team gets *Destination* and *Travel Dates*.

You don't configure anything. It learns your business from your chats.

### Custom Properties (admin-defined)

When you want a specific field, admins can add it in a few clicks from **Team Inbox → Settings**:

1. Click **+ Add Property**
2. Choose the type — **Text/Number** or **Dropdown**
3. Write a short **description** of what to capture (this is how the AI understands it)

From then on, the AI fills that property in on every relevant chat, going forward.

### AI Filter

Search and filter your **entire inbox** by any AI property — default or custom.

- *"Show every hot lead with no objection left."*
- *"Every chat with high urgency waiting on a reply."*
- *"All deals interested in the Team Plan."*

Instead of scrolling hundreds of conversations, the right ones surface in one click.

### Automatic CRM Sync

Every property — auto-created, default, or admin-defined — is **pushed straight into the CRM** on the matching contact or deal record.

- Supported CRMs: **HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho** (and Eazybe's broader integration set).
- No copy-paste, no manual logging — the CRM reflects what's actually happening in the chat.
- Keeps records current in real time as conversations evolve.

It sits inside the tools your team already uses daily — nothing new to open, no migration.
