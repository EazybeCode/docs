---
title: "View Broadcast Analytics"
description: "Review campaign details, recipient delivery activity, and message failure reasons."
---

Open a campaign's analytics to see its message, source, delivery totals, and the latest status for each recipient. You can filter, search, and export the delivery activity.

<Warning>
  The campaign names, phone numbers, dates, and delivery totals in these screenshots are examples. Your analytics page shows data from the selected campaign.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign analytics">
    On **Broadcasts**, find the campaign and select **View Analytics**. Eazybe opens the detailed report for that campaign.

    ![Broadcasts page with View Analytics buttons highlighted](/images/broadcasts/view-broadcast-analytics.jpeg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the campaign summary">
    At the top of the report, confirm the broadcast name and status, **WABA number**, **Sent on** date, **Total contact**, and **Broadcast source**. The message card shows the template preview.

    ![Broadcast analytics page with campaign summary, message preview, and Delivery activity](/images/broadcasts/broadcast-campaign-summary.png)

    <Note>
      Some source-specific details appear only when they apply. For example, a HubSpot workflow broadcast can show the recipient object and a link to the workflow.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the delivery activity">
    In **Delivery activity**, select **Total**, **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read**, or **Failed**. Each row shows the contact, **Message time**, and latest **Status**. Failed rows include a failure reason when one is available.

    ![Delivery activity with Total, Sent, Delivered, Read, and Failed tabs and recipient statuses](/images/broadcasts/broadcast-delivery-activity.jpeg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or export the results">
    Use **Search contacts** to find a phone number. Select **Export** to download the delivery group that is currently open, such as **Export Total** or the failed group.

    ![Broadcast analytics page with campaign summary, Search contacts, and Export Total highlighted](/images/broadcasts/broadcast-search-and-export.jpeg)

    <Tip>
      Open **Failed** and export that group when you need to correct contact data or share failure details with your team.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choose 10, 25, or 50 rows per page to move through the recipients. Select **Back to broadcasts** when you are finished.
