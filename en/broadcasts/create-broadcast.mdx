---
title: "Create a new broadcast"
description: "Choose a template, add recipients, review the estimated cost, and send a WhatsApp campaign."
---

Open **Broadcasts** and select **New broadcast**.

<Frame caption="Start creating a broadcast by selecting New broadcast">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/broadcast-workflow-start.jpeg"
    alt="Eazybe Broadcast home page with the New broadcast button highlighted"
  />
</Frame>

## 1. Choose your message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the broadcast">
    Enter a clear internal name, such as **August renewal reminder**. Recipients do not see this name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send from">
    Choose the WhatsApp Business number that will send the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    Search for and select an approved WhatsApp template. The message preview updates automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Enter a broadcast name, choose the sending number, and select an approved template">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/create-broadcast-choose-message.png"
    alt="Create broadcast page showing the broadcast name, sending number, template selection, and message preview"
  />
</Frame>

## 2. Choose recipients

Select one option:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upload a list" icon="file-arrow-up" href="/en/broadcasts/upload-contacts">
    Upload a CSV or Excel file and select contacts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HubSpot contact list" icon="hubspot" href="/en/broadcasts/hubspot-contact-list">
    Select a HubSpot list. This option appears only when HubSpot is connected.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Frame caption="Choose between uploading a list and selecting a HubSpot contact list">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/create-broadcast-choose-recipients.jpeg"
    alt="Choose recipients section showing the Upload a list and HubSpot contact list tabs"
  />
</Frame>

## 3. Review and send

The footer shows the selected recipient count and estimated cost after the template and recipients are ready.

Select **Send broadcast** to submit the campaign.

<Frame caption="Review the selected recipients and estimated cost before sending">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/create-broadcast-review-and-send.jpeg"
    alt="Create broadcast page showing a selected recipient, estimated cost, and Send broadcast button"
  />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Check the template, variable values, recipient count, and wallet balance before sending. A submitted campaign cannot be recalled from this page.
</Warning>
