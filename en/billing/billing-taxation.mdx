---
title: "Add Billing and Tax Details"
description: "Verify GST information for eligible INR payments and future tax invoices."
---

Use **Billing & taxation** to save the business details used for eligible INR purchases and invoices. Verify the GSTIN carefully so the registered name, state, and billing address are associated with the correct business.

<Note>
  **Billing & taxation** is available to eligible workspace admins using India-based billing. If the tab is not visible, confirm the workspace billing country and your permissions.
</Note>

## Add GST Details

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Billing & taxation**.
2. Enter the 15-character GSTIN and complete verification.
3. Review the registered company name, state, and address returned by the verification.
4. Add a finance email if invoices should be sent to a separate address.
5. Save the billing information.

<Warning>
  Check the GSTIN and registered business details before saving. Incorrect tax information can appear on future eligible invoices and can require support to correct.
</Warning>

## Review GST During a Purchase

For an eligible INR credit purchase, Eazybe shows the GST breakdown before payment. Review the subtotal, GST, and total at checkout, then complete the payment only when the information is correct.

Saved GST details can be used to create a business tax invoice for future eligible purchases.

<Info>
  Whether a purchase or invoice qualifies for Input Tax Credit depends on your circumstances. Consult your tax professional for tax eligibility or accounting advice.
</Info>

## If Verification Fails

- Confirm that the GSTIN contains 15 characters and has no extra spaces.
- Check that the GSTIN belongs to the business that should appear on the invoice.
- Retry after correcting the value.
- If a valid GSTIN still cannot be verified, contact Eazybe Support before making the purchase.
