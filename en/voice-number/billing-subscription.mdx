---
title: "Billing and Subscription for Voice Numbers"
description: "Understand the subscription, number rental, wallet balance, and usage charges for Voice Number features."
---

Voice Number uses two billing areas: your subscription and your wallet. The subscription covers the Eazybe plan and purchased-number rental; the wallet covers usage such as calls and messages.

<Info>
Billing options and amounts depend on your role, wallet currency, plan, number of seats, billing period, and the numbers in your workspace. Administrators see more billing controls than other users.
</Info>

## What You Pay For

| Item | Where to review it | Billing behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eazybe subscription | **Settings → Billing → Your plan** | Plan price depends on plan, seats, billing period, and currency. |
| Voice-number rental | **Settings → Billing → Your plan** and **CALLS → Phone Numbers → Your numbers** | Each purchased number has a recurring rental amount. A separate virtual-number subscription row may appear in **Your plan**. |
| Number setup or purchase | **CALLS → Phone Numbers → Review your purchase** | A one-time setup or purchase fee may apply, with tax where applicable. |
| Calling and SMS usage | Wallet balance and usage records | Calls and messages are charged separately from number rental. |

<Warning>Buying a number, recharging the wallet, or sending an SMS can create a real charge. Review the displayed amount and billing schedule before confirming payment or sending a message.</Warning>

## Check Your Subscription

1. Open **Settings** in the Eazybe sidebar.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Open **Your plan**.

The page can show your plan name, validity date, seats, price per seat, total price, billing period, GST notes for INR pricing, and a separate monthly row for virtual numbers.

Only administrators can see **Your plan**. If you do not see it, ask a workspace administrator to review the subscription.

### Change the plan

Select **Upgrade**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, review the available plans, set the phone-number count when shown, and select **Upgrade Now**. Complete payment in the checkout window.

<Note>Available plans and prices load dynamically. The checkout amount can change with the selected plan, billing period, phone-number count, seats, and currency.</Note>

## Check Voice-Number Rental

Open **CALLS → Phone Numbers → Your numbers**. The table shows each number's **Monthly amount**, **Purchased** date, and **Renews** date. The purchase review also shows the amount due today, rental period, one-time setup, tax, and recurring amount after the current period.

Cancelling a number keeps it active until the paid period ends. After that, the number is released and cannot be recovered. Cancel before the 28th to avoid the next monthly charge when applicable.

## Add Wallet Credits

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Credits**.
2. In **Add credits**, choose a suggested amount or enter an amount within the displayed range.
3. Review **New balance**.
4. Select **Complete purchase** and finish checkout.

The supported limits in the billing guide are:

- INR wallets: **₹1,000–₹50,000**, paid through Razorpay; GST information and tax summary may appear.
- Other wallet currencies, including USD: **10–10,000**, paid through Stripe.

Credits are added after payment is completed and the payment webhook processes the transaction.

## Use Auto Payment When Available

Auto payment is shown only for USD wallets. Expand the **Auto payment** card and turn on the switch. You can edit **Top up when below** and **Amount to add**, then select **Save changes**.

For USD wallets, each amount must be between **$10** and **$10,000**. The threshold must be at least **$1** and lower than the amount to add.

To stop automatic recharges, turn off the switch and confirm **Disable auto payment**. A saved configuration then appears as **Paused** and can be enabled again. Disabling auto payment does not remove the current wallet balance or reverse earlier recharges.

## Review Wallet History

Administrators with Credits access can open **Wallet history**. Use **Status**, **From Date**, and **To Date** to filter transactions. The available status values include **All Status**, **Completed**, **Pending**, and **Failed**.

Completed recharges may show **Download invoice** or **View payment receipt**. Free-credit records and incomplete transactions do not show invoice actions.

## Common Questions

### Why can I use Phone Numbers but not see billing controls?

Billing tabs are role- and account-scoped. **Your plan** is administrator-only. Ask an administrator to review the subscription or wallet.

### Why is Auto payment missing?

Auto payment controls are displayed only for USD wallets.

### Why is a call or SMS blocked?

Calling can be blocked when the workspace has no eligible subscription or insufficient wallet balance. SMS requires an active voice number and may incur messaging charges. Ask an administrator to check the plan and wallet.

### Where can I update GST details?

For INR administrators, open **Settings → Billing → Billing & taxation**. Enter and verify the 15-character **GSTIN**, review the company and billing details, optionally add a **Finance Email**, and select **Save Billing Information**.
