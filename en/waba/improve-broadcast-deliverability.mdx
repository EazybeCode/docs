Use these points before and after each broadcast to help more messages reach the right customers.

## 1. Monitor Your Phone Number's Quality Rating

- Check the sending number's **Quality rating** in WhatsApp Manager.
- Aim to maintain **High (green)** quality. If it becomes **Medium (yellow)** or **Low (red)**, review recent audiences, content, and sending frequency.
- Investigate negative customer feedback before expanding your next campaign.

**Related guide:** [Check and Improve Phone Number Quality Rating](/en/waba/phone-number-quality-rating).

## 2. Send Only to Customers Who Expect Your Messages

- Use contacts who agreed to receive messages from your business; avoid purchased lists.
- Explain the type of updates customers will receive and how often.
- Remove opted-out contacts before the next campaign.

**Related reference:** [Meta's guidance on opt-in and message quality](https://eventsmanager.facebook.com/business/learn/lessons/whatsapp-business-policy-compliance).

## 3. Keep Your Contact List Accurate

- Check country codes and missing or incorrect phone numbers.
- Remove duplicate records and correct invalid recipient details reported by earlier broadcasts.
- Remember that valid formatting does not confirm that a number has an active WhatsApp account.

**Related guide:** [Upload Contacts](/en/broadcasts/upload-contacts).

## 4. Use Relevant, Approved Templates

- Choose an approved template that matches the message's purpose. Promotional messages belong in the Marketing category.
- Make your business recognizable and give the customer one clear next action.
- Check the template's current status before sending; resolve paused or disabled templates first.

**Related guide:** [Template Guidelines](/en/waba/templates/guideline) · [WhatsApp marketing messaging guide](https://whatsappbusiness.com/blog/marketing-messaging-quick-guide/).

## 5. Check Personalization, Links, and Media

- Use the customer's language and relevant details rather than generic promotional text.
- Populate every required variable and check fallback values.
- Review the preview, destination links, and attached media before sending.

**Related guide:** [Template Variables](/en/broadcasts/template-variables).

## 6. Control Sending Frequency and Respect Opt-Outs

- Coordinate campaigns across your team to avoid overlapping messages to the same customers.
- Choose an appropriate local send time and reduce frequency when customers stop engaging.
- Provide a clear opt-out method. If you ask customers to reply STOP, ensure your team or configured workflow processes it.

**Related links:** [Meta's quality and frequency guidance](https://eventsmanager.facebook.com/business/learn/lessons/whatsapp-business-policy-compliance) · [Review Opt-Outs in Analytics](/en/broadcasts/view-analytics#check-opted-out-contacts).

## 7. Review Messaging Limits and Account Status

- Review the current limits and any restrictions in WhatsApp Manager before planning a large campaign.
- Phone-number quality, template status, and messaging limits are different checks; inspect each one.
- Do not assume a larger contact list means every recipient can be reached in one campaign.

**Related guide:** [Phone Number Quality Rating](/en/waba/phone-number-quality-rating).

## 8. Set a Competitive Bid Amount to Help Improve Delivery

- The **bid amount**, shown as **Bid cap** in Eazybe, is the maximum you are willing to pay for one delivered marketing message.
- A higher, more competitive bid amount may improve delivery rates and help your broadcast reach more recipients by allowing a higher delivery price when needed.
- Open **Templates**, select your marketing template, and click **Set max price** to enter your bid cap. If a cap is already saved, use the **Max … / delivery** button to review or adjust it.
- Choose an amount within your budget. For example, a bid cap of **₹1.00** allows up to **₹1.00 per delivered message**; the actual cost may be lower. This is an illustration, not a recommended bid.
- Review the campaign's maximum delivery exposure before sending. The bid cap is a per-message limit, not your total campaign budget.
- Compare actual cost and delivery results before adjusting the cap. A higher bid does not guarantee delivery or resolve every failure.
- Eazybe supports maximum pricing for eligible accounts with MM Lite enabled in INR and USD.

**Related guide:** [Set a Maximum Bidding Price](/en/waba/templates/max-bidding-price).

## 9. Review Failures Before Retrying

- Open the campaign's **Delivery activity**, select **Failed**, and read **FAILURE REASON**.
- Correct invalid contact data or template variable errors before sending again.
- Follow the guidance for the reported restriction instead of repeatedly resending the same campaign.
- Use **Export Failed** when you need to review affected contacts together.

**Related guides:** [View Broadcast Analytics](/en/broadcasts/view-analytics) · [Troubleshoot Broadcasts](/en/broadcasts/troubleshooting).

## 10. Test and Compare Results

- Start a new campaign approach with a smaller, relevant audience.
- Change one factor at a time, such as content, timing, or bid cap.
- Compare delivery, customer responses, opt-outs, and costs after allowing time for status updates.
- In Eazybe's recipient tabs, **Delivered** and **Read** are separate groups. Include both when assessing how many recipients received the message; **Sent** alone is not proof of receipt.

**Related guides:** [View Broadcast Analytics](/en/broadcasts/view-analytics) · [Customize and Export Reports](/en/broadcasts/customize-and-export-reports).

## Ready to Send?

- Confirm your recipient list, template, variables, wallet balance, and pricing.
- Follow [Create a New Broadcast](/en/broadcasts/create-broadcast).
- For unresolved delivery issues, email [support@eazybe.com](mailto:support@eazybe.com) with the campaign name, sending number, send date, and failure reasons.
