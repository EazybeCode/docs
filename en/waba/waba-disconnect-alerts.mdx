title: "Set Up WABA Disconnect Alerts"
description: "Choose who receives an email when a WhatsApp Business number disconnects and reconnect the number from WABA settings."
---

Set up disconnect alerts so the people you choose receive an email when a WhatsApp Business number loses its connection to Meta. You can manage recipients, check your numbers, and reconnect a disconnected number from **Settings → WABA**.

<Note>
Only workspace admins can change disconnect alerts or reconnect a number. Managers and employees can open the page, but the recipient list is blurred and marked **Admin only**.
</Note>

## Set up disconnect alerts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WABA settings">
    Go to **Settings** and select **WABA** in the left menu. Under **Notifications**, you will see the **Disconnect alerts** card.

    The **Connected numbers** section below it shows the connection status of your workspace's WhatsApp Business numbers.

    ![WABA highlighted in Settings with disconnect alerts and connected numbers visible](/images/waba-disconnect-alerts/open-waba-settings.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check that disconnect alerts are on">
    Disconnect alerts are enabled by default for every workspace. The **Disconnect alerts** card shows **Turn off** when alerts are on.

    <Note>
    Your organization owner's email address is automatically added as the default recipient.
    </Note>

    If alerts were previously turned off, select **Turn on** at the top right of the card to enable them again. The screenshot below shows this off state. While alerts are off, the card reads *Alerts are off. Turn them on to choose who gets notified.*

    ![Turn on button highlighted on the Disconnect alerts card in WABA settings](/images/waba-disconnect-alerts/turn-on-disconnect-alerts.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add an email recipient">
    Select **+ Add email** to add another recipient. Choose a recipient in either of these ways:

    - Select a teammate under **From your workspace**. Scroll through the list or type to filter by name or email address.
    - Type an email address and press **Enter** or select **Add**.

    Repeat this action for each person who should receive an alert.

    <Note>
    Recipients do not need to be workspace members. You can add a shared inbox such as `alerts@yourcompany.com`.
    </Note>

    <Note>
    You can save up to **10 recipients**. When you reach the limit, **+ Add email** is replaced by **10 of 10 recipients added**. Remove a recipient to make room for another.
    </Note>

    ![Email recipient field and workspace teammate list in WABA disconnect alerts](/images/waba-disconnect-alerts/add-email-recipient.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your recipients">
    The first two recipients appear as chips on the card. If you have added more than two, select **+N more** to open the full list.

    In the full list, search by name or email address and use the **×** button to remove a recipient.

    <Note>
    Changes save immediately. There is no separate **Save** button.
    </Note>

    ![Saved recipient chips, the more recipients menu, and Add email on the Disconnect alerts card](/images/waba-disconnect-alerts/review-recipients.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the alert email

When a number loses its connection to Meta, every recipient on your list receives the same alert email. Your recipient list applies to the whole workspace, rather than to individual numbers.

<Warning>
A disconnected number cannot send or receive messages until it is reconnected. Open **Settings → WABA** to check its status and reconnect it.
</Warning>

## Reconnect a disconnected number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the disconnected number">
    In **Settings → WABA**, scroll to **Connected numbers**. Each number has a **Connected** or **Disconnected** badge. Disconnected numbers appear first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Reconnect">
    Select **Reconnect** next to the number showing **Disconnected**. This opens the same Meta sign-up window used by **Connect to API** on the Channels page.

    <Note>
    You must be a workspace admin to reconnect a number.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the Meta connection flow">
    Follow the Meta sign-up flow using the same WhatsApp Business account you use when connecting through the Channels page.

    Once Meta confirms the connection, the list refreshes and the number's badge returns to **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage recipients and turn alerts off

To remove a recipient from the full list, select **+N more**, find the recipient, and select **×**. When you remove someone from a list of 10, **+ Add email** becomes available again.

<Note>
While alerts are on, you must keep at least one recipient. If you try to remove the last one, you will see *Keep at least one recipient while disconnect alerts are on. Turn the alerts off instead.*
</Note>

To stop alert emails, select **Turn off** on the **Disconnect alerts** card. Your recipient list stays saved, so you can select **Turn on** later without rebuilding it. Adding recipients, removing recipients, and turning alerts on or off each save automatically.

## Who can use disconnect alerts

| Action | Admin | Manager | Employee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open WABA settings | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| View the recipient list | Yes | Blurred and marked **Admin only** | Blurred and marked **Admin only** |
| Add or remove recipients | Yes | No | No |
| Turn alerts on or off | Yes | No | No |
| Reconnect a number | Yes | No | No |

## Troubleshooting

### WABA is missing from Settings

Reload the page. Every role can open WABA settings, but the menu appears after your workspace permissions finish loading.

### You see an invalid email message

If you see *Enter a valid email address.*, check the address for a missing `@` or domain, correct it, and try again.

### You see a duplicate recipient message

If you see *\<name\> is already on the list.*, that email address is already saved. Check the recipient chips or select **+N more** to view the full list.

### Your recipient change does not save

If you see *Could not save disconnect alert recipients. Please try again.*, the change did not reach Eazybe. The list returns to its previous state. Check your internet connection and retry the change.

### Nobody received an alert

Check the following:

- The card shows **Turn off**, which means alerts are currently on.
- At least one recipient is saved.
- Recipients have checked their spam folders.
