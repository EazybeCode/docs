Use the broadcast rate list to plan your WhatsApp campaign budget before sending messages. It lists country-wise marketing and utility template rates effective **1 October 2026**, with separate pages for INR and USD billing.

## Overview

Your broadcast estimate depends on the recipient's destination, the category of your approved template, and your messaging account's billing currency. Each rate list includes the country or territory, its ISO code, and the rate per delivered message for these two categories:

| Template category | Common examples |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing | Offers, promotions, and product announcements |
| Utility | Order confirmations, payment updates, and delivery notifications |

Use the category assigned to your approved template when looking up a rate. If your campaign reaches customers in multiple countries, look up each destination separately.

## Choose Your Billing Currency

Open the rate list that matches your messaging account's billing currency. Both pages cover the same destinations, so you can find rates for international recipients in either currency.

<Note>
Choose the currency your account is billed in, regardless of where the recipient lives. For example, an INR-billed account uses the INR rate list even when sending to customers outside India.
</Note>

## Estimate Your Broadcast Cost

1. Open the rate list for your billing currency.
2. Find the recipient's country or territory.
3. Select the marketing or utility rate for your approved template.
4. Multiply that rate by the expected number of billable delivered messages.
5. For multiple destinations or template categories, calculate each separately and add the amounts.

**Estimated message cost = billable delivered messages × applicable rate.**

Each currency page includes a worked example and details about billing exceptions. Use those details when deciding which deliveries to include in your estimate.

<Warning>
The rate list helps you estimate message charges. Account for applicable taxes, subscription charges, and other fees separately when planning your total budget.
</Warning>
