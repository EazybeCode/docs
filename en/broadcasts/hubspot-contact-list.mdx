---
title: "Broadcast to a HubSpot Contact or Deal Segment"
description: "Choose a synced HubSpot contact or deal segment, review eligible contacts, and prepare your WhatsApp broadcast."
---

Use **HubSpot segment** to choose recipients from a saved HubSpot contact or deal segment. Contact segments use their member contacts; deal segments use the unique contacts associated with their member deals.

<Info>
  **HubSpot segment** appears when HubSpot is connected to your Eazybe workspace.
</Info>

## Before You Begin

- Connect HubSpot to Eazybe with permission to read segments (`crm.lists.read`) and the relevant CRM records.
- Prepare a contact or deal segment in HubSpot. For a deal segment, associate the intended contacts with its deals.
- Give recipient contacts an international phone number in HubSpot's **Mobile phone number** or **Phone number** property.
- Choose a connected WhatsApp Business number and an approved template on **Create broadcast**. See [Create a New Broadcast](/en/broadcasts/create-broadcast).

<Note>
  **Contacts** and **Deals** identify the records in the segment; **Active segment** and **Static segment** describe how its membership is maintained.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The screenshots show examples captured from Eazybe. Segment names, record counts, and eligible-contact totals will differ in your workspace. Violet arrows and outlines highlight the controls to use.
</Warning>

## Use a Contact Segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open HubSpot segments and choose Contacts">
    Open **Broadcasts**, select **New broadcast**, and complete your message details. In **Choose recipients**, select **HubSpot segment**, then **Contacts**.

    ![HubSpot segment tab, Contacts tab, and contact-segment search highlighted with violet arrows](/images/hubspot-segments/contacts-browse.png)

    Enter a segment name in **Search HubSpot contact segments** to narrow the cards. Each card shows the segment name, its type, and its contact count. Use the left and right page arrows below the cards when more results are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a contact segment and check its eligible contacts">
    Select the segment card. The card is highlighted, and a summary appears above **HubSpot segments**. Wait for **Resolving eligible contacts…** to finish, then check the eligible-contact count.

    ![Selected contact segment with 2 eligible contacts of 4 resolved and its highlighted card](/images/hubspot-segments/contacts-selected.png)

    In this example, the segment contains **4 contacts**, but the summary shows **2 eligible contacts of 4 resolved**. Use the eligible-contact total when reviewing your audience, rather than assuming every member can receive a message.

    <Note>
      Eazybe checks `mobilephone` first, then `phone`. Contacts without a usable phone number are excluded, and duplicate phone numbers are removed. An eligible number does not guarantee successful WhatsApp delivery.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a Deal Segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Deals and find your segment">
    In **Choose recipients**, select **HubSpot segment**, then **Deals**. Use **Search HubSpot deal segments** to find the segment you want.

    ![Deals tab and Search HubSpot deal segments highlighted above cards showing deal counts](/images/hubspot-segments/deals-browse.png)

    The cards now show **Deal segment** and the number of **deals** in each segment. Use the page arrows below the cards to browse additional results.

    <Note>
      Switching between **Contacts** and **Deals** clears the current segment selection and search. Select the segment you want after switching.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a deal segment and review its associated contacts">
    Select the deal-segment card and wait for the summary to finish resolving contacts. The summary states: **Recipients are unique contacts associated with deals in this segment.**

    ![Selected deal segment showing 10 deals on its card and 6 eligible contacts in its summary](/images/hubspot-segments/deals-selected.png)

    In this example, **10 deals** resolve to **6 eligible contacts**. Eazybe sends to the associated contacts' phone numbers. The deal count is not the recipient count: deals can share contacts, have several associated contacts, or have no associated contacts.

    <Note>
      A contact associated with multiple deals is not counted as a separate recipient for every deal. Duplicate phone numbers are removed. Check the eligible-contact summary before reviewing the cost.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand Segment Membership

| Label | How membership works |
| --- | --- |
| **Active segment** | HubSpot adds or removes records as they match or stop matching the segment's filters |
| **Static segment** | Membership changes when records are manually added or removed |

To prepare a contact segment first, see [Create a HubSpot Segment](/en/integrations/hubspot/create-segment).

## Personalize and Review Your Broadcast

Select your approved **Template message** and check the **Message preview**. If the footer says **Choose a template to calculate recipients**, choose a template before reviewing the final audience and cost.

### Map Workspace Template Variables

When you select a **Workspace variable template** and a HubSpot segment, use **Map template variables to HubSpot** to personalize each numbered variable.

The selected segment determines which properties are available:

| Selected segment | Available mapping properties |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | **Contact properties** from HubSpot |
| **Deals** | **Deal properties** from HubSpot |

For each variable, open **Choose a HubSpot property**, use **Search HubSpot properties…** to find the field, and select it. Enter a **Fallback value (required)** for every variable. Eazybe uses that fallback when the selected property is empty.

For example, use a contact's first name when working with a contact segment, or a deal's name when working with a deal segment. With a deal segment, the message uses the mapped deal values and is sent to the associated contacts.

<Frame caption="workspace_variable_new with a deal segment: choose a deal property and enter a fallback for all three variables.">
  <img
    src="/images/hubspot-segments/workspace-variable-new-mapping.png"
    alt="workspace_variable_new mapping panel with three numbered variables, Deal properties, property pickers, and required fallback fields highlighted in violet"
  />
</Frame>

The **workspace_variable_new** example shows the fields before mapping. Complete all three property selections and fallback values before sending. When you choose a contact segment instead, these fields show **Contact properties**.


<Note>
  The property type follows your segment selection. After switching between **Contacts** and **Deals**, select the segment and review every variable's mapping and fallback before sending. These selections apply only to this broadcast; saved template mappings are not changed.
</Note>

### Review HubSpot Template Variables

Select your HubSpot variable template in **Template message**, then choose a HubSpot segment. Review the suggested values in **Map template variables to HubSpot** and adjust the property or fallback where needed.

<Frame caption="hubspot_fallback with a contact segment: review the suggested City and Country/Region mappings and their fallbacks.">
  <img
    src="/images/hubspot-segments/hubspot-fallback-mapping.png"
    alt="hubspot_fallback mapping panel showing City and Country/Region contact properties with call_fallback and country_fallback values"
  />
</Frame>

In this **hubspot_fallback** example, the first variable maps to **City** with **call_fallback**, and the second maps to **Country/Region** with **country_fallback**. These are the example template's suggested values; review them and enter fallback wording that fits your message.

**Zoho variable templates** with numbered variables also show this panel when a HubSpot segment is selected. See [Template Variables and Personalization](/en/broadcasts/template-variables).


When your message and recipients are ready, review the recipient count and **Estimated cost** in the footer. Follow [Estimated Cost and Sending](/en/broadcasts/estimated-cost-and-sending) to finish.

<Warning>
  **Send Broadcast** sends a real campaign and uses wallet balance. Check the audience, template variables, and cost before sending. Active segments can change as HubSpot updates their membership.
</Warning>

## Troubleshoot Segment Selection

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **HubSpot segment** is missing | Connect HubSpot to the current Eazybe workspace, then reopen the broadcast page |
| **No matching HubSpot contact segments** or **No matching HubSpot deal segments** | Clear the search or try another name. Check that you selected the correct **Contacts** or **Deals** tab |
| **No contact segments found** or **No deal segments found** | Check that the connected HubSpot account has a segment of that record type. Allow HubSpot to finish processing it, then refresh Eazybe |
| **HubSpot list access required** or **Reconnect HubSpot to continue** | Select **Reconnect HubSpot**, grant the requested access, then return and select **Try again** |
| **Unable to load HubSpot segments** | Select **Try again**. If the error persists, check the HubSpot connection under **Integrations** |
| **This deal segment contains no associated contacts.** | Associate contacts with the deals in HubSpot, or choose a different deal segment |
| **Associated contacts exist but have no usable phone number.** | Add or correct the contacts' international mobile or phone numbers in HubSpot, then refresh and select the segment again |
| **HubSpot is still updating this segment.** | Wait for HubSpot to finish, refresh the page, and select the segment again |

<Tip>
  A card's contact or deal count describes its HubSpot membership. The selected summary shows the contacts Eazybe can resolve for sending. Check both when a segment's recipient total is smaller than expected.
</Tip>
