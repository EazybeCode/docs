---
title: "Lead Response Limit"
description: "Control how many new contacts your AI agent handles versus your human sales team"
---

The Lead Response Limit setting lets you control the ratio of new incoming contacts your AI agent handles automatically. Instead of the AI responding to every new contact, you choose how many it picks up — the rest are left for your sales team to handle manually.

This is useful when you want to compare AI performance against your human team on the same lead pool, or when you're onboarding with Eazybe and want to start with a smaller share before going all-in.

## Where to Find It

Go to your agent's **Settings** tab (gear icon in the left sidebar). The Lead Response Limit section sits between the **WhatsApp Profile** name-source selector and the **Blocked Numbers** section.

![Lead Response Limit in Agent Settings](/images/lead-response-limit/image1.png)

## How to Set It Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Toggle">
    In the Settings tab, find the **Lead Response Limit** section and turn the toggle on. When it's off (the default), the AI handles every new contact as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Ratio">
    Two number fields appear: **AI handles [X]** out of every **[Y] new contacts**. Enter your desired ratio. For example, setting X = 1 and Y = 5 means the AI picks up 1 out of every 5 new contacts, and the remaining 4 are left for your team.

    ![Set Your Lead Response Ratio](/images/lead-response-limit/image2.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Setting">
    A yellow info banner below the fields confirms your ratio in plain language — for example, "AI will handle 1 out of every 5 new contacts." Review it to make sure it matches your intent, then you're all set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens to Each Contact

- **Contacts picked up by the AI** go through the full conversation flow — qualification, follow-ups, and handoff — exactly as they would without this setting.
- **Contacts not picked up by the AI** receive no automated reply. They appear in your inbox like any normal WhatsApp lead, ready for your sales team to handle manually.

<Note>This setting only applies to **new, inbound contacts**. Existing conversations that are already active with the AI continue uninterrupted regardless of the ratio.</Note>

## Changing the Ratio

You can update the ratio at any time. Changes take effect from the **next batch** of new contacts — they don't retroactively reassign contacts that were already allocated. A lead already in conversation with the AI won't suddenly go silent, and a lead already assigned to your team won't get an unexpected AI message.

If you turn the toggle off, the AI resumes handling all new contacts from that point forward. Contacts that were in the "human" bucket during the active period remain unaffected.

## Example Scenarios

- **Starting conservative:** Set 1 out of 10 for the first week, review AI performance in the Analytics tab, then increase to 3 out of 10 once you're confident.
- **High-volume filtering:** Set 9 out of 10 to let the AI handle the bulk of initial qualification while your closer focuses on the hottest leads.
- **A/B testing:** Set 1 out of 2 for an even 50-50 split, then compare booking rates, response times, and qualification accuracy between AI and human cohorts over a fixed period.
