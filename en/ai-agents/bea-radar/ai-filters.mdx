---
title: "AI Filters"
description: "Narrow the Team Inbox to the conversations that need attention, using what BEA Radar has already read from them."
---

AI filters narrow the chat list by the values BEA Radar has written on your conversations. You can filter by any AI property: the sales brief fields such as intent and urgency, the properties created for your business, and the ones an Admin has added.

Filters can be stacked, and they work alongside the standard chat filters, so a broad list can be reduced to a specific set of conversations.

<Note>
  **Requirements:** at least one AI property must have a value on your chats. A property added a moment ago has no values yet and returns an empty list until conversations are analysed against it.
</Note>

## Where to Find It

The **AI filters** control sits above the chat list in the Team Inbox, next to the standard filters.

## Filtering Your Inbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Filters">
    Click on **"AI filters"** to filter your chat list by an AI property.

    ![AI filters button above the chat list](/images/bea-radar-9.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an AI Property">
    Select the AI property you want to filter by, for example **"Conversation State"**.

    ![Selecting the Conversation State AI property](/images/bea-radar-10.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Property Value">
    Select the property value, for example **"Waiting on Rep"**.

    ![Selecting the Waiting on Rep property value](/images/bea-radar-11.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add More Filters">
    Add further filters the same way to narrow the list.

    ![Combining multiple AI filters](/images/bea-radar-12.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Combining Filters

Multiple AI filters can be applied at once, and each one narrows the list further. AI filters can also be combined with the standard chat filters, such as channel or assignee, in the same view.

## Example Filters

| To find | Filter on |
|:--------|:----------|
| Hot leads with nothing left blocking them | Intent, combined with an empty Objection |
| Time-sensitive chats awaiting a reply from your team | Urgency, combined with Conversation State |
| Everyone interested in a particular plan | The business property recording plan interest |

## Behaviour to Be Aware Of

- **Filtering only changes what is shown.** Filters do not delete, move, or edit conversations.
- **A chat appears once the property has a value.** Conversations where the property is empty are not returned.
- **Stacked filters narrow, they do not widen.** Two filters that exclude one another return an empty list.
