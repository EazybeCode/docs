Team Inbox uses AI to organize conversations, maintain customer context, help segment the inbox, and show when an automated agent is handling a customer.

<Note>
  AI labels, AI profile intelligence, and AI properties require an eligible plan. They are hidden on Free and Starter plans. Eazybe identifies Scaler, AI Basic, and higher plans as eligible for AI Properties. Some AI sections are also hidden by role or access scope.
</Note>

## Use AI labels

Eligible non-Employee users can expand **AI labels** in Lists.

| Label | What to use it for |
|---|---|
| **Hot Deals** | Focus on conversations identified as strong sales opportunities. |
| **Escalation** | Review conversations that need priority handling. |
| **Follow Up Needed** | Find customers who need another action or response. |
| **Unreplied** | Find conversations still waiting for a reply. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an AI label">
    Expand **AI labels** in Lists and click **Hot Deals**, **Escalation**, **Follow Up Needed**, or **Unreplied**.

## Take over from an AI agent

When an AI agent is actively handling the selected customer, a banner appears above the composer with the agent's name.

- Select **Take over** to pause that AI agent for the customer and continue as a human.
- While the AI is paused, select **Return to AI** to let the same agent resume handling the customer.
- If Team Inbox detects that multiple AI agents have sent to the same customer, it shows a warning and pauses the involved agents to prevent competing replies.

The message history records the transition from AI to a teammate where applicable.

## Rephrase a draft with AI

Write at least 10 characters, then select the round AI control in the composer or supported Quick Reply editor. Eazybe rephrases the text and replaces the draft. Review the result before sending or saving.

## Why an AI feature may be missing

- The workspace is on Free or Starter.
- The signed-in user has the Employee role.
- No AI labels or deployed AI-agent stages have data in the current scope.
- No AI-property definitions are configured.
- The selected customer has not produced enough information for a value.
- The feature is limited by team, channel, or member permissions.
