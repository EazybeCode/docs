Eazybe credits are funds stored in your workspace wallet and used for paid WhatsApp API activity. From the **Credits** tab, you can check your current balance, add credits whenever needed, or use auto payment to keep your wallet funded automatically.

## Open Credits

Go to **Settings → Billing → Credits**.

The page displays:

- Your current wallet balance
- Auto-payment status and settings, when available for your wallet
- Quick amounts and a custom amount field for one-time purchases

<Note>
Access to Credits depends on your workspace permissions and eligible Eazybe services. Ask your workspace admin if the tab is not visible.
</Note>
