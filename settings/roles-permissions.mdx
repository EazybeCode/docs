## Roles & Permissions

Every person in your Eazybe organization has one of four roles. The role decides which parts of the workspace they can open and whose data they see.

| Role | Who it is for | Sees data for |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| **Owner** | The person who created the organization | The whole organization |
| **Admin** | People who run the workspace with the Owner | The whole organization, except the Owner |
| **Manager** | Team leads | Their own team |
| **Employee** | Agents and reps handling chats | Themselves |

<Info>
The Owner can do everything an Admin can, and the Owner's own chats and activity stay private: no other role can see them, including Admins. The Owner label appears under **Settings → Profile** for the person who created the organization. Anyone who joins an existing organization starts as an Employee.
</Info>

### What each role can do

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Owner">
    The Owner is the person who created the organization.

    - Has full access to every section of the workspace
    - Can do everything an Admin can do
    - Sees chats and activity for everyone in the organization
    - Keeps their own chats and activity private from every other role
    - Access cannot be restricted by anyone

    To move ownership to another person, contact us at **hey@eazybe.com**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Admin">
    Admins have full access to every section, and their access cannot be restricted.

    - See staff, chats and analytics for everyone except the Owner
    - Assign and change roles, and set working hours
    - Manage the plan, seats, billing and wallet history
    - Connect and disconnect WhatsApp Business API numbers
    - Turn Team Inbox on or off
    - Connect CRMs and control CRM sync settings
    - Decide what Managers and Employees can open

    Admins cannot see the Owner's chats or activity.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manager">
    Managers work at team level.

    - See staff, chats and analytics for their own team
    - Create and manage teams and team members
    - Connect and disconnect HubSpot, Zoho and Salesforce
    - Use Broadcasts, Templates, AI Agents and Credits
    - Adjust access for Managers and Employees in their team

    Managers cannot change roles, manage the plan or billing, or connect WhatsApp Business API numbers.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Employee">
    Employees work on their own conversations.

    - Open Team Inbox on the chats assigned to them
    - Use Broadcasts, Templates and AI Agents
    - Edit contact properties in the Mini CRM
    - See their own profile and their own activity

    Employees cannot open Teams, Analytics, Channels or Credits unless an Admin or Manager gives them access.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Assigning a role

Only an Owner or Admin can assign or change a role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Staff">
    In the workspace sidebar, click **Staff**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or edit a person">
    Click **Add Employee** to invite someone new. For an existing person, open the **⋯** menu on their row and click **Edit Employee Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the role">
    In **Employee Role**, select **Admin**, **Manager** or **Employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Send Invite** for a new person, or **Save Changes** for an existing one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can have more than one Admin. A pending invite cannot be edited until the person accepts it.
</Note>

### Customizing access with Members & Access

On the **Scaler** plan, you can choose which sections Managers and Employees can open. Go to **Settings → Members & Access**.

- **By role** sets the default for everyone with that role.
- **By member** overrides the default for one person. Click **Reset to role default** to remove the overrides.

These are the defaults:

| Section | Owner / Admin | Manager | Employee |
|---------|---------------|---------|----------|
| Team Inbox | Always on | Always on | On |
| See all channels | Always on | On | Off |
| Credits | Always on | On | Off |
| Channels | Always on | On | Off |
| AI Agents | Always on | On | On |
| Broadcasts | Always on | On | On |
| User Properties | Always on | On | On |
| Sync Settings | Always on | On | On |
| Analytics | Always on | On | Off |
| Members & Access | Always on | On | Off |

<Warning>
Owner and Admin access is always on and cannot be switched off, so no one can lock themselves out of permissions.
</Warning>

Turning a section on lets a person open it. It does not widen whose data they see, and it does not unlock Admin-only actions such as changing the plan or connecting a WhatsApp Business API number.

### FAQs

**Can an Admin see the Owner's chats?**

No. The Owner's chats and activity are visible only to the Owner.

**Can a Manager see an Admin's or the Owner's chats?**

No. Managers see chats for Managers and Employees in their team.

**Can an Employee see other people's chats?**

No. Employees work on the chats assigned to them.

**Who can change my role?**

An Owner or Admin in your organization. Your role is shown under **Settings → Profile**.

**Why can't I see Analytics or Channels?**

These sections are off by default for Employees. Ask an Admin or your Manager to turn them on for you.
