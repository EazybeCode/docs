---
title: "Purchase or Manage a Plan"
description: "Review your Eazybe plan, purchase an upgrade, and manage seats or subscription billing."
---

Open **Settings → Billing → Your plan** to see the current plan, billing period, validity, seat count, and price per seat for your workspace. From the same tab, eligible admins can upgrade the plan or manage the existing subscription.

<Note>
  **Your plan** is available to workspace admins. If you cannot see it, ask an admin in the current workspace to review the plan for you.
</Note>

## Purchase or Upgrade

1. Open **Settings → Billing**, then select **Your plan**.
2. Select **Upgrade**.
3. Choose the plan and the monthly or yearly billing option offered on your screen.
4. Review the total price, billing period, and number of seats.
5. Continue to the secure checkout and complete the payment.
6. Return to **Your plan** and confirm that the plan details have updated.

<Warning>
  Selecting **Upgrade** can change the workspace subscription and create a charge. Check the plan, billing period, seats, currency, tax, and total before completing checkout. GST is added to eligible INR purchases.
</Warning>

## Manage an Existing Plan

The actions shown on **Your plan** depend on the subscription and billing country:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit seats** | Adds or removes seats when seat management is available for the plan |
| **Upgrade** | Changes the plan or billing period using the available purchase flow |
| **Manage Subscription** | Opens the Stripe billing portal for subscription billing actions |

<Note>
  **Manage Subscription** is shown only for customers outside India. India-based customers can use the plan and seat actions displayed in Eazybe or contact Eazybe Support for billing help.
</Note>

## Confirm the Change

After checkout, review **Your plan** again. If the previous plan is still displayed, refresh the page and check the payment status before starting another purchase.

<Warning>
  Do not submit the same purchase again while the first payment is pending. If payment succeeded but the plan has not updated, contact Eazybe Support with the workspace name, payment reference, and purchase time.
</Warning>
