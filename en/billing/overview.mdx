## Access and Currency

- Billing tabs and actions depend on your workspace permissions, country, wallet currency, and Eazybe services.
- **Your plan** and **Wallet history** are available to eligible workspace admins.
- Auto payment is currently available for USD wallets.
- INR purchases can include GST and may use a different checkout flow from other currencies.

<Note>
  If a tab or action described in this section is not visible, confirm that you are in the correct workspace and ask a workspace admin to check your access.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Review the currency, amount, tax, billing period, and payment method before confirming a purchase. Billing actions can charge a saved card or send you to a secure checkout.
</Warning>
