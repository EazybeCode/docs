## When the estimate appears

Eazybe calculates the estimate after you select:

- A WhatsApp Business number
- An approved template
- At least one valid recipient

While the request is processing, the footer displays **Calculating...**. When complete, it shows the valid recipient count and estimated cost.

<Info>
  The displayed value is an estimate. The final charge can depend on the template category, recipient country, and WhatsApp pricing.
</Info>

## HubSpot estimate fallback

Eazybe normally validates contacts in the selected HubSpot list. If HubSpot temporarily applies a rate limit, Eazybe can show an approximate estimate based on:

`Segment size × unit price`

The interface identifies an approximate estimate when exact recipient validation is unavailable.

## Checks before sending

Eazybe confirms that:

- A broadcast name is entered
- A number and approved template are selected
- At least one valid recipient is available
- Required variables or HubSpot mappings are complete
- The wallet has sufficient balance

Select **Send broadcast** only after the recipient count and estimate are ready.

<Warning>
  Do not close or refresh the page while the send request is being submitted.
</Warning>
