Eazybe Voice Number gives your team a business phone number for calling and messaging. You can make individual calls, run an Auto Dialer session, purchase and manage numbers, review call activity, analyze results, and read SMS verification codes in one place.

<Info>
Voice Number uses your wallet for call and SMS usage. Review the billing page before making calls or sending messages.
</Info>

## Get started

1. Open **CALLS → Phone Numbers** and buy a number or open **Your numbers**.
2. Open **CALLS → Dialer** to make an individual call.
3. Open **CALLS → Auto Dialer** to upload a contact list and call contacts in sequence.
4. Use **CALLS → Call History**, **Call Analytics**, and **SMS** to review activity.
5. Open **Settings → Billing → Wallet history** to review wallet charges.

<Warning>Calls and SMS can create real wallet deductions. Confirm the selected voice number and review the displayed billing information before continuing.</Warning>
