Select **View Analytics** beside a campaign to open its detailed report.

## Campaign summary

The top section shows:

- Broadcast name and status
- WhatsApp Business number
- Sent date
- Total contacts
- Broadcast source
- Template preview

<Frame caption="Review campaign details, the message preview, and delivery activity">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/broadcast-campaign-summary.png"
    alt="Broadcast analytics page showing the campaign summary, message preview, and delivery activity table"
  />
</Frame>

## Delivery activity

Use the status tabs to review:

- **Total**
- **Sent**
- **Delivered**
- **Read**
- **Failed**

Each row shows the contact, message time, and latest delivery status. Failed rows also show the failure reason.

<Frame caption="Review recipient message times and delivery statuses">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/broadcast-delivery-activity.jpeg"

  />
</Frame>

## Search and export

- Use **Search contacts** to find a phone number.
- Select a status tab to filter the report.
- Select **Export** to download the currently selected delivery group.

<Frame caption="Search campaign contacts or export the selected delivery group">
  <img
    src="/images/broadcasts/broadcast-search-and-export.jpeg"
    alt="Broadcast analytics page with the Search contacts field and Export Total button highlighted"
  />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Export the **Failed** tab when you need to correct recipient data or investigate delivery problems with your team.
</Tip>

## Pagination

Choose 10, 25, or 50 contacts per page. Select **Back to broadcasts** to return to the campaign list.
