## Find a Broadcast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the campaign filters">
    Use **Search broadcasts** to find a campaign by name. Select a WhatsApp Business number, **All sources**, or **All statuses** to narrow the list.

    ![Broadcasts page with Search broadcasts, WhatsApp number, All sources, All statuses, and Refresh](/images/broadcasts/broadcast-home-overview.png)

    <Tip>
      Select **Refresh** when a recent campaign is still processing or its delivery totals have changed.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the campaign analytics">
    Find the campaign row and select **View Analytics**. Eazybe opens the campaign summary and its **Delivery activity** table.

    ![Broadcasts page with View Analytics buttons highlighted](/images/broadcasts/view-broadcast-analytics.jpeg)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the Campaign Row

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Broadcast** | Campaign name, source, and template |
| **Total** | All recipients currently represented by the delivery statuses |
| **Sent** | Messages accepted for sending but not yet delivered |
| **Delivered** | Messages delivered but not yet marked as read |
| **Read** | Messages marked as read by recipients |
| **Failed** | Messages that could not be delivered |
| **Date** | Date the campaign was sent |
| **Status** | The campaign's current state, such as **Completed** or **Failed** |
| **View Analytics** | Opens recipient-level campaign details |

## Understand the Broadcast Source

The source below the campaign name tells you where the broadcast originated. Sources can include:

- **Workspace**
- **HubSpot segment**
- **HubSpot workflow**
- **HubSpot contact**
- **Zoho**
- **Salesforce**

## Move Through Older Campaigns

Choose 25, 50, or 100 rows per page, then use the page controls to move through the campaign history.
