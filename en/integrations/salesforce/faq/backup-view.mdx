With **Back up in activities** enabled, Eazybe saves **1:1 and group conversations as completed Tasks** on the associated Salesforce **Contact or Lead**. Messages are appended to the conversation’s daily activity as backups run.

## Find a saved conversation in Salesforce

1. Open the associated **Contact** or **Lead** record in Salesforce.
2. Open **Activity** and find the **WhatsApp Chat by Eazybe** entry for the date you want.
3. Open the completed Task and read the synced messages in **Description**.

See the setup steps and examples in [Backup in Activities](/en/integrations/salesforce/activity-backup).

## When activity backup is off

With this setting off, Eazybe uses the existing Salesforce backup route: the **WhatsApp Chats by Eazybe** custom object, plus **Notes & Attachments** for Contacts. Enabling activity backup sends subsequent chat backups to completed Tasks instead of that route.

### Salesforce Contact's Page

Synced WhatsApp conversations appear in two locations within respective Salesforce Contacts' Pages:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Notes and Attachments">
    Find your synced conversations in the Notes and Attachments section.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WhatsApp Chats by Eazybe">
    View conversations in the custom activity object.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

![Contact Page - Notes and Attachments](/images/1196813393.png)

![Contact Page - WhatsApp Chats Activity](/images/1196813635.png)

### Salesforce Lead's Page

Synced WhatsApp conversations display in the **"WhatsApp Chats by Eazybe"** custom activity object within respective Salesforce Leads' Pages.

![Lead Page - WhatsApp Chats Activity](/images/1196813835.png)

<Tip>
  Both Contacts and Leads pages provide easy access to your synced WhatsApp conversations, helping you maintain a complete communication history within Salesforce.
</Tip>
