EazyBe Team Inbox is now integrated directly into Bitrix24, allowing your team to manage WhatsApp conversations without leaving the CRM.

## Team Inbox on Contact Page

### Step 1: Find the Team Inbox Tab

On any contact's page in Bitrix24, look for the Team Inbox tab.

### Step 2: Open Team Inbox

Click the Team Inbox tab to view conversations with that specific contact.

## Global Team Inbox

### Step 1: Find the EazyBe Team Inbox

Look for the EazyBe Team Inbox option in the left sidebar of Bitrix24.

Click to access the full Team Inbox showing **all conversations** across your team.

## Requirements

To use the Team Inbox integration, you need:

- **Install EazyBe app** from the Bitrix24 Market
- **Active EazyBe integration** with Bitrix24

<Tip>
If you have a API or CoEx number connected, you can send and receive messages directly from the Team Inbox within Bitrix24.
</Tip>

## Access Control

Team Inbox accessibility is role-based:

- **Admins**: Can view all team conversations
- **Employees**: Can only view conversations assigned to them

## Features

### View Conversations
- **Contact inbox**: See all WhatsApp conversations with a specific contact
- **Global inbox**: View all team conversations in one place
- **Real-time sync**: Messages sync automatically every 12 minutes

### Send Messages
If you have a connected API or CoEx number:
- Send messages directly to contacts from Bitrix24
- No need to switch between applications
- All messages are logged in the contact's timeline

### Team Collaboration
- **Unified inbox**: All team conversations in one place
- **Contact context**: Access inbox while viewing contact details
- **Seamless workflow**: Stay within Bitrix24 while managing WhatsApp
