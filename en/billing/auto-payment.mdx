Auto payment keeps your wallet funded by charging a saved card when your balance reaches the threshold you set.

<Note>
Auto payment is currently available for **USD wallets**.
</Note>

## Turn on auto payment for the first time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Auto payment">
    Go to **Settings → Billing → Credits** and expand **Auto payment** if it is collapsed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the toggle">
    Switch **Auto payment** on. If you do not have an existing auto-payment setup, Eazybe connects it to the card already saved for your Eazybe subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the settings">
    Confirm **Top up when below** and **Amount to add**. The status changes to **Auto payment active** when setup is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- **Top up when below** is the wallet balance that triggers auto payment.
- **Amount to add** is the amount charged to your saved card and added to the wallet.
- The threshold must be lower than the amount to add.

For example, set the threshold to USD 5 and the amount to add to USD 50. Eazybe starts auto payment when the balance reaches USD 5 or less. After a successful payment, the balance increases by USD 50.

## Change the amounts

1. Click either amount box or its pencil icon.
2. Update the threshold and amount to add.
3. Click **Save changes**.

For USD wallets, the threshold must be at least USD 1, the amount to add must be at least USD 5, and each value can be up to USD 10,000.

## Change the payment card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Change card">
    In the Auto payment panel, click **Change card** beside the saved card details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the card in Stripe">
    Enter the new card details on the secure Stripe page and complete the setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the card">
    Return to Billing and confirm that the last four digits shown in Auto payment match the new card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Eazybe displays only the card brand and last four digits in the workspace.

## Turn auto payment off or on again

- To stop automatic charges, turn off the toggle and confirm **Disable auto payment**.
- Disabling stops future automatic top-ups but keeps the saved configuration, so it can be turned on again later.
- The status on the Billing page always shows whether auto payment is active.

## Emails and notifications

Auto-payment updates are sent to the email address of the workspace account. Eazybe also creates an in-app notification for these events.

| Event | What you receive |
| --- | --- |
| Payment started | An email with the amount and transaction ID |
| Payment successful | An email with the amount, transaction ID, and updated balance when available |
| Payment failed | An email with the failure reason, retry timing, and a link or instruction to update the payment method |
| Repeated failures | After three failed attempts, auto payment is disabled and a final email is sent |

<Note>
Manually turning auto payment on or off updates the status in Billing. Activity emails are sent for payment attempts and for automatic disabling after repeated failures.
</Note>
