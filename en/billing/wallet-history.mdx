Use **Wallet history** to review credit purchases and wallet usage from one place.

<Note>
The **Wallet history** tab is available to workspace admins with Credits access.
</Note>

## Find a transaction

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Wallet history">
    Go to **Settings → Billing → Wallet history**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Filter by **Completed**, **Pending**, or **Failed**, or choose a date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    Check the date, description, credit or debit amount, balance after the transaction, invoice, and payment status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download an invoice">
    When an invoice is available, use the download action in that transaction row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the list does not load, click **Refresh**. If a successful payment is still missing after refreshing, contact Eazybe Support with the payment details.
