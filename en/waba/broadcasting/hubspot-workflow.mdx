---
title: "HubSpot Workflow for WhatsApp Campaigns"
description: "Choose the Eazybe WhatsApp template action you want to use in a HubSpot workflow."
---

Use the Eazybe integration to automatically send WhatsApp messages from HubSpot workflows. Choose the workflow action that matches how you want contacts to interact with your campaign.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Send Ready-to-Use Template" icon="paper-plane" href="/en/waba/broadcasting/hubspot-workflow/send-ready-to-use-template">
    Send a pre-approved WhatsApp template when a contact meets your HubSpot workflow criteria.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send Wait and Reply Template" icon="reply" href="/en/waba/broadcasting/hubspot-workflow/send-wait-and-reply-template">
    Send a WhatsApp template and continue the workflow based on the contact's reply.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Both options require an active HubSpot account with workflow access and the WhatsApp Integration by Eazybe installed.
</Info>
